FAQ

Common Questions

Are you insured and background checked?expand_more
Yes. Alafia Cleaning is fully insured and every team member is background checked before joining, so you can feel comfortable welcoming us into your home or business.
Do I need to be home during the cleaning?expand_more
Not at all. Many clients set up secure access and go about their day. Whatever you are most comfortable with, we will work around it with clear communication.
Do you bring your own supplies?expand_more
Yes, we arrive with the supplies and equipment needed to get the job done. If you prefer specific products for your home, just let us know and we will accommodate where we can.
How do I book or reschedule?expand_more
Booking is easy through our quote form, by phone, or by email. Need to move a visit? Just reach out and we will find a new time that works for you.
What if I am not happy with the clean?expand_more
Your satisfaction matters to us. If something is not right, let us know within 24 hours and we will make it right. That is our satisfaction guarantee.
